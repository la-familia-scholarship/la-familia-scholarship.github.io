The four initiatives

Four things,
this summer.

In motion

College visit — the lead

A general, same-day campus visit for the younger cohort — student-led tour, dorms, lunch. Illinois State as the model. ~2-hour drive, two cars, ~six to eight once chaperones are counted. ~$1,500 already in hand for this trip.

  • Done: floated at the gym — warmly received; three scholars confirmed
  • Done: the one previously-unmet parent now met in person
  • Date target: July 6 or 7 (week after the holiday); backup ~July 13–14
  • Path chosen: the small-group visit ($12/person) — guide, group kept together, buffet
  • Confirm July 6 vs. 7 with the scholars, then lock & register
  • Reach the possible 4th scholar (no contact info yet)
Researching

A summer trip

A chaperoned overnight to bond the group and get them outdoors. Lead location: Mecan River Outfitters & Lodge (WI, ~2h40m) — cabins, on-site meals, canoeing nearby. The one initiative likely to carry real dollars — it anchors the year’s fundraising goal.

  • Mario calls for an early-August availability + deposit feeler (~$250 may hold dates)
  • Keep Kickapoo as the backup location
  • Possible canoeing day (4–6 hrs) folded into the budget
  • Target early August, before school
In reserve

A game together

A live Chicago game — low cost, easy to trigger. 500-level tickets ~$13–20. Penciled in for whenever a gap opens between the other events; can roll into the fall.

  • Mario calls a ticketing contact about group / organization rates
In motion

Washington send-off

Sponsor two scholars’ selective university summer program (depart July 26) — formalwear and travel-packet items, through a controlled, documented spend. Housing, meals, and transport are provided; the committee’s job is outfitting.

  • Done: mechanism decided — an Amazon wish list (priority order) ordered through a clean entity account; the invoice is the record; ~$300 cap per scholar
  • Attire: business-professional ×2; business-casual Mon–Thu (no jeans)
  • Scholars order by next week — a 3–4 week cushion for exchanges
  • Pedro stands up the entity Amazon account
