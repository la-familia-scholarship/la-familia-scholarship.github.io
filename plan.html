A general daily campus visit (not the open house) for the younger cohort — student-led tour, faculty access, lunch. Illinois State or U of I. Six young men tentatively committed; confirming with individuals and securing parent approval.

  • Float it to the young men at the gym — Thu June 18

  • Confirmations in by June 25
  • Lock the date & call the school by June 26
  • Confirm with all six young men individually
  • Secure parent approval from all attendees

Researching

A summer trip

A chaperoned overnight outside Chicago — White River WI, the Michigan dunes, and similar on the table. Activities plus unhurried time together.

  • Everyone brings 1–2 location options next meeting
  • Target early August, before school
  • Trip-specific consent addendum once dates/location land
  • Confirm insurance coverage before committing
In reserve

A game together

A live Chicago game — low cost, easy to trigger. Held until the first two initiatives land; can roll into the fall if needed.
