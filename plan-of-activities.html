Status board

Where the work stands.

Capturing every gift. Giving has already begun organically — friends and family are contributing as committee members talk to them. The most urgent operational step is moving from informal to formal: a true ledger, donor records, receipts going out. In motion.

Setting up the payment path. The LLC is being instantiated. A Chase account opens under the LLC. Phase 1 donations route through Zelle to that account, with a committee member providing the details and following up with a receipt. In motion.

Canonical artifacts and member access. The strategy, the one-pager, this plan, and the workshop history all live at a stable hub the committee can reach from any phone. Live now.

Running the ask. The committee carries the ask through their networks — friends, family, and people who believe in this work. Active.

Scholarship application. A draft application is in development from the Chairperson's template — a one-page form plus a handwritten goals page, signed by the young adult as their own commitment. In drafting.

Locking the three initiatives. The summer game, the trip, and the college visits all need their final dates and budgets settled. Underway.

Public donation site (v1). A simple landing page describing the work with instructions to contact a committee member to donate — also the warm-lead capture mechanism. Next up after the close.

The moments

Summer execution.

After the close, the work shifts to the moments themselves — the reason all of this exists.

A game together (June – early July). The cohort to a live game in the Chicago area.

College visits (June – early July). Campus visits — walking a campus changes what feels possible.

A summer trip (anytime through the summer). A trip outside Chicago, chaperoned by the committee. For many of the seven, the first trip of its kind.
