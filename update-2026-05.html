The origin

Ten years.
One court.

For over a decade, Mario and many of you have been showing up at the boathouse to play basketball — recreational, regular, real.

Young people started coming through. And between the older men running the court and the younger ones picking up the game, something began to happen — mentorship, collaboration, support flowing in both directions.

Word of mouth keeps bringing new young adults through. The love of the game and of community keeps evolving. And here we are.

Basketball is the front door. Powerful mentorship is the next room.

La Familia is not the founding of something new. It is the formalization of something this community has been doing — together, quietly, consistently — for more than a decade.

Who we serve
7

Seven young people.
Identified. Known. Earned.

These are not aspirational picks. The seven are the young people who've shown up year after year — on the court, in the moments that matter — with consistency and earnestness.

They span high school to college. Each one is known to Mario by name, by family, by the path they're walking. As new kids prove their consistency, the circle grows.

How we show up

More than money.
Presence.

La Familia disburses support through three channels — and shows up through three shared experiences this summer.

The scholarship channels
01

Cash to parents

For students in high school or in college. Parents direct funds toward what their student needs most.

02

Cash to students

Typically for students already in college and managing their own affairs. Direct, trusted, no intermediaries.

03

Direct to vendors

For specific costs — college visit travel, books, program fees. The scholarship pays the vendor directly.

The funding model

The ask is $50.

Small enough to be a low barrier. Meaningful at scale. From friends and family who believe in this work.

$50
Default ask
$1,000
Ceiling, year one
$2,500
Baseline target

We cap individual gifts at $1,000 this founding year — deliberately. We're a learning year. The ceiling is not a limit on belief; it's a scaling constraint. A promise to ourselves that we won't over-extend before we're ready.

Better to do right by seven young people than to scale before we're ready.

Friends-and-family model. Not tax-deductible this year. Donors sign off acknowledging that — and we close the loop with every one of them.
